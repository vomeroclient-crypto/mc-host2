NEW COMMANDS
------------
  /listfarms [type]
      Lists all registered farm schematics with their placed/unused status.
      Optional filter: kelp, bamboo, cobblestone, misc.
      No permission required (staff and players can see the list).

  /unusedfarms
      Shows every farm that has NOT been placed in the world yet.
      No permission required.

  /addfarm <key> <filename.litematic> [type] [description]
      Dynamically register a NEW farm from any .litematic already sitting
      in plugins/DonutSMPWorld/schematics/. The farm is immediately available
      via /spawnfarm and /listfarms.
      Requires: donutsmp.admin
      Example: /addfarm sugarcane Sugar_Cane_Farm.litematic misc "Sugar cane farm 64 wide"

  /spawnfarm <key>
      Paste a farm schematic at your current standing position.
      The farm is marked as "placed" so /unusedfarms no longer lists it.
      Requires: donutsmp.admin, must be a player (not console)

NEW CLASSES
-----------
  FarmManager.java
      Owns the FARMS registry (separate from SCHEMATICS so stashes and farms
      don't get mixed up), tracks which farms have been placed, and handles
      all farm command logic.
